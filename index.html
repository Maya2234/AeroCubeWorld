About this project

What this is

A small WebGL scene built without a 3D library: geometry, matrices and shading are all written directly against the raw WebGL API.

How it is built

One cube is copied into a grid and translated to its cell and scaled in y, color assigned per corner. Perspective and target matrices place the camera; the fragment shader blends the corner colours and lights every face. Level 2 replaces the noise and palette with a point-filtered sample from height and color png files. Level 3 draws the bubbles, clouds, butterflies and windmills from their own buffers with a per-object model matrix.

Levels

Each level isolates one idea:
1) heights and per-vertex colour
2) heights and colours sampled from assets/color-map.png and height-map.png,
3) clouds, butterflies and bubbles moving along cubic bezier curves.
Orbit moves the camera around the origin point.

Performance

The landscape is one buffer drawn in a single call, so it stays smooth up to tens of thousands of cubes. The readout shows the resolution, cube count, rebuild time and frame rate. Decreased performace is possible with a slower GPU.
