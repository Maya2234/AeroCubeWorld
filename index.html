About this project

What this is

A small WebGL scene built without a 3D library: geometry, matrices and shading are all written directly against the raw WebGL API.

How it is built

One unit cube of 36 vertices is copied into a grid, each copy translated to its cell and scaled in y, with a colour per corner. Perspective and look-at matrices place the camera; the fragment shader blends the corner colours and lights every face.

The tabs

Each tab isolates one part of Level 1: 1a translation into a grid, 1b/c heights and per-vertex colour, 1d the camera orbiting the centre. The grid buttons change the resolution without changing the landscape.

Performance

The landscape is one buffer drawn in a single call, so it stays smooth up to tens of thousands of cubes. The readout shows the resolution, cube count, rebuild time and frame rate; a reasonably fast GPU helps at the top settings.
